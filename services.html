OUR METHODOLOGY

A Series of Well-Managed Processes

A candidate’s commencement of work with you is never considered an event to be taken lightly — it is the outcome of a disciplined, thorough process developed and refined over more than four decades.

01

Position Specifications

The key to each successful placement is a thorough understanding of your needs. A detailed briefing session establishes an exact picture of your organisation, the role, and the culture we must match.

02

Search Strategy

Operating in an ever-changing environment, most placed candidates come not just from our database. Our extensive network of sources is accessed to identify prospective candidates for opportunity evaluation.

03

Candidate Assessment

Candidates are interviewed and evaluated to ascertain they fit with your organisation’s culture — beyond technical qualifications, we assess values, behaviour, and leadership style.

04

Shortlist Presentation

The finest choices of talent are shortlisted for presentation after a stringent filtering process. You receive a concise, high-quality slate — not a long list of maybes.

05

Interview & Evaluation

Interviews are arranged between you and the candidates. Their responses are evaluated after each interview, and we provide guidance throughout the selection process.

06

Reference Checks

Thorough reference checks are conducted and you are advised of the outcome — our structured process goes beyond standard employment confirmation to give you real intelligence.

07

Negotiation & Offer

We handle negotiation of the appropriate compensation package for the selected candidate, and ensure they resign and do not accept a counter-offer from their current employer.

08

Onboarding Follow-Up

Though the search assignment concludes, we maintain contact with both the candidate and employer to pave the way for a comfortable, compatible, and lasting relationship.

TALENT PROFILING TOOLS

Science-Backed Candidate Assessment

Besides searching for the right candidate, our unique talent recruiting methodology provides the most timely, accurate, and effective executive search solutions to our clients. We use validated psychometric and competency tools to ensure every hire is not just technically competent — but a true fit for your culture and leadership requirements.

WPI

Work Personality Index

Personality & Work Performance

The Work Personality Index is designed to measure personality traits that relate directly to work performance and work environment. Useful in both selection and development, it focuses on the normal adult population and answers three critical hiring questions:

  • Which skills is this individual naturally good at?
  • What does the individual genuinely enjoy doing at work?
  • How does the individual typically behave in a professional setting?

It helps you identify key motivators and behaviours — essential for hiring the right people, developing employees, and supporting great leaders.

Enquire About WPI →
ERI

Employee Reliability Inventory

Behavioural Reliability & Risk

Created by Harvard specialists, the Employee Reliability Inventory measures whether a candidate’s future behaviour is likely to be affected by any of six critical reliability factors:

  • Employment Stability — Long-term commitment potential
  • Courtesy — Commitment to service with internal and external stakeholders
  • Productivity — Consistent contribution to results
  • Emotional Maturity — Responsibility, good judgement, teamwork, and tolerance for frustration
  • Trustworthiness — Reliability and integrity
  • Safe Behaviour — Awareness and accident prevention

CCV

Sales Competence Coefficient

Sales Aptitude & Effectiveness

The Sales Competence Coefficient (CCV) is a specialised assessment designed to measure the core competencies that predict sales performance and effectiveness. It is validated through real-world company trials and provides a data-driven picture of a candidate’s commercial aptitude.
