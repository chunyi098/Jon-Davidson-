⚠  Important Notice

Protecting Yourself from
Recruitment Scams

We are aware of fraudsters impersonating JonDavidson consultants to deceive jobseekers. Your safety is our priority — please read this page carefully.

🚨

JonDavidson has not been compromised

We want to assure all candidates and clients that JonDavidson’s systems, data, and infrastructure remain fully secure. Scammers are independently impersonating our brand and consultants — they have no affiliation with us whatsoever. We are actively monitoring and working to report these fraudulent activities.

Warning Signs: How to Spot a Scam

Fraudsters are becoming increasingly sophisticated. Here are the most common red flags to watch out for:

  • !
    You receive an unsolicited message on WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS or social media (e.g. LinkedIn, Facebook) claiming to be from a JonDavidson consultant offering you a job.
  • !
    The offer sounds too good to be true — high pay for minimal effort, work-from-home tasks, or “guaranteed” income with no interview required.
  • !
    You are asked to pay a fee, deposit, or purchase items (e.g. gift cards, vouchers) as part of a “job requirement” or “training programme.”
  • !
    The sender’s email does not end in @jondavidsongroup.com. Scammers often use Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo, or slightly altered domains (e.g. jondavidsons.com, jon-davidson.net).
  • !
    You are asked to provide sensitive personal or financial information — such as your bank account number, NRIC, or passport details — before any formal job offer or interview.
  • !
    The LinkedIn or social media profile of the supposed consultant has few connections, no work history, a generic photo, or was created recently.
  • !
    You are pressured to act quickly, sign documents immediately, or keep the “opportunity” confidential.

How JonDavidson Will & Will Not Contact You

Use this as your checklist whenever you receive a message claiming to be from us.

✓  JonDavidson will
  • Contact you from an official @jondavidsongroup.com email address
  • List roles on our website, RecruitCRM, JobStreet or MyCareersFuture
  • Conduct proper interviews before making any offer
  • Provide a signed offer letter on company letterhead
  • Ask only for standard information relevant to your application
  • Be listed on the MOM EA licence register under EA Licence No. 22S1412
✗  JonDavidson will never
  • Ask you to pay any fee, deposit, or purchase gift cards
  • Request your banking details, CPF, or full NRIC before a formal offer
  • Guarantee a job with no interview or selection process
  • Contact you only via WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal social media
  • Ask you to transfer money for any reason
  • Offer unusually high salaries for simple or vague tasks

How to Verify a JonDavidson Consultant

If you have received a message and are unsure whether it is genuine, here is what you can do:

  • 1
    Ask the person to send you an email from their official JonDavidson address. All genuine consultants use @jondavidsongroup.com.
  • 2
    Check our Our Team page to see if the consultant’s name appears. If they are not listed, treat the contact with caution.
  • 3
    Call our Singapore office directly on +65 6295 7155 to confirm whether the consultant works with us and has contacted you.
  • 4
    Check the Ministry of Manpower’s EA licence register to verify JonDavidson’s licence (EA Licence No. 22S1412).

If You Have Been Targeted

If you believe you have been contacted by a scammer impersonating JonDavidson, or if you have already suffered a financial loss, please take the following steps immediately:

  • ✓
    Do not transfer any further money or share additional personal information.
  • ✓
    Report the incident to the Singapore Police Force via the iWitness portal or by calling 999 (emergency) or 1800-255-0000 (non-emergency).
  • ✓
    Report the scam at ScamAlert.sg, the National Crime Prevention Council’s official scam reporting portal.
  • ✓
    Contact your bank immediately if you have made any payment, and request a transaction reversal or account freeze.
  • ✓
    Let us know by emailing partners@jondavidsongroup.com so we can track and report the fraudulent activity.
